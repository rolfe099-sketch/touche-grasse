# Changelog

Notable user-facing changes are recorded here. Development previews are not public App Store releases.

## Unreleased

### Food logging and recipes

- Multi-ingredient typing and speech commands, bilingual matching and remembered confirmed products.
- Explicit raw/cooked and dry/cooked distinctions, reviewed barcodes and editable local nutrition-label recognition.
- Whole-meal corrections, selected dates, quick repeats and persistent Undo.
- Recipes, finite saved portions, custom serving counts and measured cooked-weight controls.
- Recipe-first Discover with 12 original meal ideas and offline illustrations.

### Planning and progress

- Named meal shortcuts, optional nutrient targets and complete-day intake summaries.
- Optional weigh-ins, observed trends and retained correction history.
- Guided goal setup, dated training/rest weekday profiles and a separate meal planner with drafts and atomic consumption.

### Reliability and distribution

- Retained speech, label, repeat, portion and planner drafts.
- Validated backup/merge restore, separate local recovery copies and protection for historical snapshots.
- Narrow-screen, keyboard and accessibility refinements; Chromium and iPhone-sized WebKit workflow coverage.
- Public privacy/support pages and first production iOS upload preparation. Public availability and App Review approval are not implied.

See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for next priorities and [documentation](docs/README.md) for behavior and validation boundaries. Git history retains the detailed implementation record.
