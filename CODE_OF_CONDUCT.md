# Code of Conduct

We want Touche Grasse to be welcoming to people with different backgrounds, bodies, abilities and levels of experience. Participation includes issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

- Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique the work, not the person.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, sexualize discussions or publish someone's private information.
- Do not shame people for their body, food choices, health, weight or fitness goals.
- Keep discussion relevant. Spam, deceptive promotion and repeated disruption are not welcome.
- Protect personal records: use fictional examples instead of diary exports, recordings or identifying photos.

Report concerns privately to [support@eriksenlabs.com](mailto:support@eriksenlabs.com). Include relevant links and context without unnecessary sensitive information. The maintainer will handle reports as privately as practical; absolute confidentiality or a response deadline cannot be promised.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation or block accounts according to the circumstances. This policy applies to the maintainer as well as contributors. Good-faith disagreement and respectful technical criticism are welcome.
