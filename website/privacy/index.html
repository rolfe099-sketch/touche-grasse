Touche Grasse Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Touche Grasse is maintained by the developer behind the Touche Grasse open-source project. Contact: support@eriksenlabs.com.

Your food and fitness records

Food entries, recipes, saved portions, meal plans, targets, optional body measurements and weigh-ins, confirmed product choices, and unfinished drafts are stored locally in the app. The app does not operate a developer-hosted account or diary synchronization service. It includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Camera, photos and labels

Camera and photo access are optional and used when you choose scanning or recipe-photo features. Nutrition-label recognition runs locally. Label images and recipe photos are not uploaded to a developer server. Selected images, recognized text and your corrections may remain in local drafts or saved recipes until removed.

Voice

When you choose Voice, the app requests microphone and speech-recognition access and uses the operating system's speech service. Recognition may use Apple's servers depending on device, language and system availability; the app does not force on-device-only recognition. Transcripts used for food commands may be retained locally as drafts. The developer does not operate a voice-processing server. See Apple's Dictation privacy information. You can use typing instead and revoke permissions in iOS Settings.

Barcode lookup and external services

If you request a product lookup, its barcode is sent to Open Food Facts. That service also receives network information such as your IP address and request headers. Your diary, weight history and meal plans are not included in that request. See Open Food Facts privacy information. Confirmed products can subsequently be reused locally.

Links you choose to open, including Buy Me a Coffee, source documentation and support links, use the relevant external service. Those services apply their own privacy practices. App diary data is not attached to those links. Privacy and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process web request information; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Backups, retention and deletion

The app keeps automatic recovery copies on the device separately from the active diary. Exported backups contain personal records, including any saved measurements, photos or drafts. You choose whether and where to share or save an export. Files saved to iCloud or another provider follow that provider's practices. Device or operating-system backups may also include app data according to your settings.

Use the app's delete, draft-discard and recovery-copy controls to remove records. Earlier recovery copies and exported files may still contain deleted information; remove those separately. Removing the app removes its local app-container data, but does not necessarily remove exported files or operating-system backups. There is no developer account to delete. We cannot recover or remotely delete records stored only on your device.

Contact and changes

Email support@eriksenlabs.com with privacy questions or applicable data-rights requests. Do not attach a diary backup unless you intend to share its personal contents. If you email us, we receive the address and information you provide and use it to respond. This policy will be updated when data practices change.
